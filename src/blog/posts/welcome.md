Hi, I'm Kaloyan, and this is the first post on my blog.

I work as an AI Solution Architect, and most of my days go into designing and building **agentic AI** and **multi-agent systems**, and helping organisations work out how AI fits into their architecture. I learn a lot along the way, and much of it never gets written down. This blog is where I want to change that.

## What to expect

Practical notes from real work, written to be useful rather than polished:

- Patterns for building reliable agents and multi-agent workflows
- Enterprise AI architecture: platforms, governance, and getting things to production
- Tooling I use day to day, such as agent instructions in `AGENTS.md` and reusable skills in `SKILL.md` files
- Lessons learned, including the things that didn't work

That's it for now. Thanks for stopping by. More soon.
