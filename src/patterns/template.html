Architecture Patterns

Software design patterns are proven answers to problems that keep coming back. Agentic AI has its own set: how to split work between models, when to let an agent loop, and how to keep the whole thing safe and affordable in a large company. These are the patterns I reach for, each with a diagram, a code sketch and the traps I’ve seen, plus how I build them on real cloud services.
